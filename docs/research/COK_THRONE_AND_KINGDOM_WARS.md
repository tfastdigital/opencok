## 1. Throne War (Battle for the Throne)
- **Location & Appearance**: Central **Palace** in the middle of the Kingdom (`(600, 600)`), larger than player castles and without a normal player level badge.
- **Occupation Win Conditions**:
  - **Classic**: Occupy the central Palace continuously for **8 hours** (24h overtime rule reappoints existing King). Troops die during Throne/Catapult combat.
  - **Modern (2026)**: Occupy continuously for **2.5 hours** to win immediately, or hold the **longest total occupation time** when the timer ends.
- **Catapults (Surrounding Turrets)**: Controlled by the alliance that most recently garrisoned each Catapult; if held by a different alliance than the Palace holder, they periodically bombard and kill defending soldiers in the Palace.
- **King Buffs & Powers**:
  - **Troops Attack +15%**, **Troops Defense +15%**, **March Capacity +10%**.
  - **Hall of the King & Kingdom Skills**: Spend Silver for **Bumper Harvest**, **Red Envelope**, **Locating**, **Kingdom Peace Shield**.
  - **Kingdom Gifts**: **5 Conqueror's Gifts**, **10 Defender's Gifts**, **20 Supporter's Gifts** (once per player per tenure).
- **16 Royal Offices / Titles**:
  - **8 Buff Titles**:
    - *Queen*: Resource Income +15%, Resource Protection +20%
    - *Prime Minister*: Build/Research/Training Speed +5%
    - *Judge*: Resource Income +10%, Training Speed +5%
    - *Financial Officer*: Resource Income +5%, Food Production -10%
    - *Knight*: Troop Attack +5%, March Speed +10%
    - *Guard Captain*: Troop Attack +5%, Troop Defense +5%
    - *Scholar*: Research Speed +10%
    - *Blacksmith*: Build Speed +10%
  - **8 Debuff / Punishment Titles**:
    - *Maid* (Attack -10%, Income -5%), *Groom* (Income -5%, Food -10%), *Clown* (March -5%, Train -10%), *Civilian* (Defense -5%, Income -10%), *Scout* (Attack/Defense -8%), *Bandit* (Protection -20%), *Criminal* (March -15%), *Fool* (Research -15%).

## 2. Kingdom Conquest / KvK (Strongest Kingdom)
- **Eligibility**: Castle Lv.20+ to teleport to the opposing kingdom's Black Belt.
- **9-Stage Strongest Kingdom**: Gather Resources, Kill Monsters, Upgrade Building, Research Technology, Train Troops, Kill Event / Conquest Stage (lowest-scoring kingdom becomes Defending Kingdom; others become Attacking Kingdoms).
- **Victory Rewards**: Personal kill-ranking rewards, substantial Gathering Bonus, higher-level resource nodes, and stripping the defeated kingdom's King & Royal Offices.

---

## 3. Dragon Campaign (Alliance Tournament)
- **Enrollment**: Alliance >= 2 weeks old, Top 50 in Kingdom, max 50 battle members (>= 3 days in alliance). Weekly 2-hour match.
- **Battlefield Rules**: No permanent troop deaths (faster free healing), shared alliance teleport pool, faster castle burn.
- **Buildings**: Point Producers (*Crystal Mine*, *Training Ground*, *Armory*, *Supply Point* — 20 camp + 20 individual pts on capture), *Teleport Point*, *Blessing Tower* (Attack/Defense boost), *Hall of Healing*, *Dragon Tower* (area attack superweapon), *Hall of Knights* (Dragon Knights).

## 4. The 5 Civilizations (Factions)
1. **Dragon-born**: T11 *Raging Crossbowman*, T12 *Pegasus Knight*, Dragon *Scartha* (Ice).
2. **Viking**: T11 *Slaughter Cavalry*, T12 *Savage Warlord*, Dragon *Kraken* (Water).
3. **Yamato**: T11 *Fearless Artillery*, T12 *Shura Archer*, Dragon *Yukine* (White).
4. **Huaxia**: T11 *Voulgeman*, T12 *Phoenix Wings*, Dragon *Kylin*.
5. **Crescent**: T13 *War Elephant* (+1% all-soldier HP per 5,000 up to +20%), Dragon *Fire Dragon*.
- **Popularity & Ancestral Spirit**: 6 tiers (*Hostile -> Neutral -> Friendly -> Respect -> Admire -> Worship*) + permanent stacking Ancestral Spirit tech trees.

## 5. Dragons & Heroes Systems
- **Dragons (Dragon Cave)**: Basic (*Black*, *Blue*, *Red*) + Civilization Dragons (*Scartha*, *Kraken*, *Yukine*, *Kylin*, *Fire Dragon*). Hatching (22h), Lv.1–40, Bond (0–100%), Guardian vs Marching Dragon, 10 Skill Slots, Dragon Talents, Dragon Travel, Dragon Words (Castle Lv.14+).
- **Heroes (Hero Palace Lv.23+ & Prison)**: 50 Blueprint Shards = 1 Hero; March, Garrison in buildings, Official Positions, Prison capture. Tavern unlocks **Dragonglass** at Castle Lv.15+.
